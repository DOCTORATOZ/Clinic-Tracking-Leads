# Clinic Tracking Leads — Consolidated Requirements

**Status:** Phase 1 implementation baseline  
**Scope:** internal Care D Clinic lead, care-coordination, follow-up and appointment operations for vascular access, dialysis care coordination and one-day surgery. It is not an EMR/EHR or a patient-marketing platform.

## 1. Product boundary

Phase 1 prevents a clinic lead from being missed after first contact. It is a
manual-first workflow tool, not an EMR/EHR: store only the symptom, service
interest and assessment details needed to triage and follow up. UI is
Thai-first.

Admin/coordinators manually receive and register leads from Facebook, LINE OA,
TikTok, Phone and configurable other channels. The same model must later
support integrations without changing core business records.

### MVP scope

- Find/create patient and create a lead/case from a configurable source.
- Assign/relay the case to a nurse for an initial call.
- Let admin record the nurse's result, preserving the nurse's work separately
  from the admin's data entry.
- Generate configurable multi-round follow-up work; seed days **1, 3, 7, 14,
  30**.
- Work queues for due, overdue and upcoming follow-ups; retries/ad-hoc tasks;
  complete history.
- Create and manage manual appointments linked back to a follow-up where one
  prompted the booking.
- Provide an in-app calendar that displays appointments and follow-up tasks,
  and synchronise eligible items to a connected Google Calendar.
- Search, basic dashboard, audit trail, staff/role/configuration management,
  multi-clinic isolation and duplicate warnings.
- Internal operational notifications for overdue follow-up, appointment work and
  calendar-sync exceptions. These are staff-facing only and never send a
  patient-facing message.

### Not in Phase 1

- Live Facebook, LINE OA, TikTok, phone/CTI, DAZ, HIS, partner, SMS
  or outbound messaging integrations, including appointment reminders and
  birthday/engagement messages.
- Automated messages, chatbot, advanced marketing attribution,
  automatic patient merges, full clinical records, billing, inventory or
  treatment workflows.

## 2. Users and attribution

| Role | Required Phase 1 capability |
|---|---|
| Admin/coordinator | Intake, search, nurse assignment, result entry, follow-up and appointment operations |
| Nurse | Performs/reports calls; login optional, but exists as staff reference |
| Manager/owner | All-clinic operational view, plans/channels/staff configuration and audit access |
| System administrator | Tenant/account technical administration; no implicit clinical-data access |

For every nurse-provided assessment/contact keep these distinct values:

```text
performed_by / reported_by = nurse who made or supplied the result
performed_at               = actual contact/assessment time
recorded_by                = admin/user who entered it
recorded_at                = entry time
```

`created_by` and `updated_by` are audit metadata, not replacements for these
workflow attributions.

## 3. Required workflow

1. Lead arrives through Facebook, LINE OA, TikTok, Phone or Other.
2. Admin searches first, then creates/selects a patient and creates a new
   lead/case with source, contact details, service interest, symptoms/concern,
   priority and notes. HN is optional.
3. Admin assigns the case to a nurse; status becomes `awaiting_nurse_call`.
4. Nurse calls/assesses and reports the outcome. Admin records the outcome on
   the nurse's behalf, retaining both nurse and admin attribution.
5. Admin selects a follow-up plan and anchor date. The system creates planned
   tasks from that plan.
6. For each task, staff records each contact attempt and its result. A no
   answer may add a retry/ad-hoc task without erasing the original task.
7. If a visit is needed, the user can save the result and create an appointment
   in one flow.
8. Appointment completion, cancellation, no-show or reschedule does not stop
   future follow-ups unless staff explicitly pauses/cancels the plan or closes
   the case.
9. At the final plan step staff records a final outcome, closes the cycle, or
   extends/selects another plan.

Suggested case states: `new`, `awaiting_nurse_call`, `contact_in_progress`,
`follow_up_active`, `appointment_scheduled`, `closed_converted`,
`closed_not_interested`, `closed_unreachable`, `closed_cancelled`,
`closed_other`. Appointment state is a separate lifecycle.

## 4. Business rules

- A patient can own multiple leads/cases. A case can own many follow-up tasks,
  results and appointments.
- Each case receives an immutable human-readable `case_number` generated by
  the system. Managers may configure its clinic-scoped format (for example a
  `CL` prefix and sequence policy); workflow users cannot type or edit it.
- Source channel is immutable except for an auditable correction. It is not the
  contact channel: a Facebook lead may be called by phone.
- The plan, not source code, defines its ordered offsets. Changing a plan must
  not mutate historical generated tasks; snapshot/version step information at
  task creation.
- Services/programs are configurable clinic reference data. Each active service
  may define a default follow-up plan for newly created cases; staff may select
  an approved alternative per case with an auditable reason. A service is not a
  case number and changing its default never changes an existing case/task.
- Calculate due dates in the clinic timezone (initially `Asia/Bangkok`), store
  timestamps in UTC, and make Day-0 anchor/date override auditable.
- A completed task needs a recorded result or explicit skip/cancel reason.
  Overdue is normally a computed state over a pending/in-progress task.
- HN is optional at intake. Normalize and warn when HN or normalized phone
  matches another patient; never auto-merge or silently overwrite an HN.
- Closed records are read-only to normal workflow actions. Corrections/reopen
  need permission, reason and audit history.
- Protect sensitive notes by least privilege. Do not expose symptoms or raw
  payloads in broad notifications/dashboard summaries.
- Store a patient’s preferred contact channel, `do_not_contact` choice and
  care-contact consent with who/when recorded. Marketing consent is separate,
  opt-in only and does not imply consent to care contact (or vice versa).
- Patient-facing reminders, campaigns and birthday messages must not be sent in
  Phase 1. Internal task notifications do not require or alter marketing
  consent.
- A future “recommendation” or re-appointment must be framed as a clinician-
  approved continuing-care recommendation with a reason and audit record; do
  not model it as automatic upsell.

## 5. Initial domain/schema contract

All tenant-owned tables include `clinic_id`, UUID primary keys, UTC timestamps,
and relevant `created_by`, `updated_by` fields. Names below are logical table
names; final SQL may use singular/plural convention consistently.

| Entity | Purpose and key fields |
|---|---|
| `clinics` | Tenant identity; name, timezone, active. |
| `users` | Supabase Auth profile + clinic membership; `auth_user_id`, display name, role, active. A membership table may support users in multiple clinics. |
| `patients` | Person identity; optional `hn`, names, normalized/display phone, optional DOB/sex, preferred channel, `do_not_contact`, consent fields. |
| `patient_external_identities` | Future provider/HIS identity; platform, scoped external account/user IDs, handle/display name, verification fields. Unique provider/account/user when user ID exists. |
| `lead_sources` | Configurable seed values Facebook, LINE OA, TikTok, Phone, Other; code, type, active, provider/account reference. |
| `services` / `service_catalog` | Configurable clinic service/program/course list; name, active, description and default follow-up plan for new cases. |
| `leads` | Service episode: patient, source, received time, service interest, symptoms/concern, priority, status, assigned nurse/owner, plan, anchor, closure fields, optional source external IDs. |
| `lead_assignments` | Assignment/reassignment history: lead, assigned staff, assigned by/at, role, ended reason/time. |
| `follow_up_plans` | Configurable clinic plan: name, active/default, version. |
| `follow_up_plan_steps` | Plan step: sequence, `day_offset`, label, active, default assignee role. |
| `follow_up_tasks` | Planned/ad-hoc work: lead, optional step, sequence, scheduled time, snapshot step fields, assignee, lifecycle/status, priority, completion/cancellation metadata. |
| `follow_up_results` | Contact/assessment attempt: lead, optional task, channel, contacted time, contact status/outcome, symptom status, summary, next action/time, `performed_by`, `reported_by`, `recorded_by`, `recorded_at`. Multiple results per task are allowed. |
| `appointments` | Patient + lead, optional source result, start/end, type, branch/provider, status, reschedule/cancel reasons, notes. |
| `operational_notifications` | Staff-only inbox items for overdue task, appointment due, calendar-sync failure or review; recipient, link, read time and no patient-facing delivery payload. |
| `activities` / `audit_logs` | Append-only actor/action/entity/before-after-safe metadata/time; separate sensitive detail by access policy. |
| `integration_events` | Inbox/audit for future webhooks/imports: provider, event/message/conversation/account/user IDs, raw restricted payload, hash, idempotency key, processing status/error, resolved entity links and retries. |

Use an immutable ID as every primary reference, never name or phone. Store
provider IDs as external identities/events, never as the patient primary key.

## 6. Follow-up and appointment requirements

Seed a Standard plan with steps Day 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30. Managers can create or
edit plans for future work without a deployment. A task can have several
attempts/results and its full history remains visible in the lead timeline.

Each result requires actual time, contact channel, contact status, outcome,
summary, next action and performer/recorder attribution. Appointment creation
copies patient and lead context and sets `source_follow_up_result_id` when it
originated from a result. A lead may have zero-to-many appointments; a result
may lead to zero-to-many appointments to preserve booking/reschedule history.

## 7. Phase 2 integration boundary

Phase 2 candidates: Facebook/Meta, LINE OA, TikTok, Phone/CTI, DAZ, HIS and
partner APIs; SMS/LINE reminders and approved automation are also deferred.
Google Calendar is the approved exception in the calendar section below. Every
provider adapter must:

1. receive/verify payloads outside the core domain;
2. write an `integration_events` inbox record with a stable idempotency key;
3. resolve identity safely (verified provider ID, approved HN/HIS mapping,
   normalized phone, then manual review);
4. call the same validated domain service used by manual intake; and
5. record success/failure/retry without duplicate domain records.

No adapter, route handler, webhook or queue worker directly inserts/updates a
lead, result or appointment table.

### 7.1 Care D future scope (explicit roadmap)

- **Channel ingestion:** verified LINE OA/Facebook/other provider webhooks may
  create an integration inbox event, then follow manual-review and duplicate
  rules. They must never create a patient or case directly.
- **Patient-facing messages:** appointment reminders, follow-up messages and
  birthday engagement require a provider adapter, explicit purpose-specific
  consent, opt-out, approved templates, quiet-hours policy, delivery log and
  retry/failure handling.
- **Documents and clinical records:** referral documents, imaging, operative
  records and attachments require a separately approved restricted-document
  module: document type, access role, retention, view/download audit and
  malware scanning. Do not place clinical documents in general case notes.
- **Continuing-care recommendations:** any recommendation for a subsequent
  service or appointment needs a clinician-approved rule/reason and auditable
  staff action. It is not a sales automation feature.

## 8. Calendar and Google Calendar synchronisation

The in-app calendar is the operational calendar for appointments and scheduled
follow-up tasks. It must offer day/week/month views and filters for event type,
status, assignee and branch, render all values in the clinic timezone, and open
the linked case/task/appointment.

An authorised manager/admin may connect one clinic Google Calendar through
OAuth, select its destination calendar and review connection/sync status. The
clinic record is the source of truth: eligible appointment and follow-up-task
changes create, update or cancel the corresponding Google event. Persist the
Google calendar/event IDs, etag/version, idempotency key, sync state, timestamps
and safe error details. Retrying must update the same event, never duplicate it.

Once the connection is configured with auto-sync enabled, a committed clinic
change must enqueue a background sync job; it must not wait for a browser tab,
server action response or manual user retry. A bounded idempotent worker claims
the job, calls the Google adapter, records the outcome and retries transient
failures with a configured limit/back-off. The UI exposes pending/failed state
and a privileged manual retry without ever treating a browser client as the
sync worker.

Google-originated edits/deletions to clinic-owned items must be recorded as
`needs_review`; they cannot silently mutate a lead, task or appointment.
Google-only events may be shown read-only and never create patient/workflow
records automatically. Tokens are encrypted/server-only; raw provider data and
tokens are never shown in the normal UI or logs.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

1. Admin manually records leads from every seeded source and can create/link a
   patient without an HN.
2. A case captures service interest and symptoms, can be assigned to a nurse,
   and visibly enters the waiting-for-nurse workflow.
3. An admin-entered nurse result shows correct performer/reporter and recorder
   plus actual and recorded times.
4. The Standard plan creates exactly five tasks at 1/3/7/14/30-day offsets;
   configurable future plan edits do not alter generated history.
5. Due, overdue and future queues are correct in Asia/Bangkok and support
   filtering/search/reassignment.
6. Multiple result attempts and appointments remain accessible in a lead
   timeline; edits never erase history.
7. A result can create a linked appointment in one save flow, and appointment
   state does not automatically cancel remaining tasks.
8. Tenant/RLS tests prove cross-clinic records are inaccessible; audit records
   capture creation, updates, state changes and privileged corrections.
9. Duplicate HN/phone produces a warning and auditable user choice, not an
   automatic merge.
10. An integration-event duplicate idempotency key cannot create a second
    patient, lead, result or appointment.
11. A scheduled follow-up task and an appointment each appear once in the
    in-app calendar and the configured Google Calendar; retries do not create
    duplicate Google events.
12. A clinic reschedule/cancellation updates the linked Google event, while an
    external Google edit remains `needs_review` until staff resolves it.
13. A staff member can see overdue-task, appointment and calendar-sync
    exceptions in an internal notification inbox without sensitive clinical
    detail or sending a patient-facing message.
14. Care-contact consent and `do_not_contact` are visible to authorised staff
    at intake and are preserved in audit-safe data; marketing consent remains
    purpose-specific and optional.
